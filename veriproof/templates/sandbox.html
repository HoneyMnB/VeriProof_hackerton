How to use this tool

For staff development verification only. It does not create a production sale.

  1. Choose a registered asset and enter its asset ID.
  2. Provide a confirmed Solana Devnet payment signature and the buyer public wallet.
  3. Run verification and inspect the seller AI, buyer AI, and network panels in order.

A successful run shows the 402 request, negotiated terms, verified transaction, and settlement result. Failed runs identify the failing stage.
